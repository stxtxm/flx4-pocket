FLX4 Pocket 0.6.3

- SYNC now locks a deck to the master's beat by itself, as rekordbox: press SYNC on a stopped deck and it lands on the beat at PLAY, start it before the master and it lands on the beat the moment the master starts, and a hot cue, a needle search or a refined grid lands on the beat again. No second SYNC press.
- A jog or a scratch is still yours while you hold it, and the beats are back together the moment you let go. A loop or a beat roll keeps playing inside itself.
- SYNC can be armed on the deck on air: its light comes on, it holds the tempo, and the other deck follows it — playing or not — so the two can be started in either order.
- Starting the deck that is already synced no longer hands the master role to it, so the pair keeps the master you picked.
