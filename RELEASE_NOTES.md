FLX4 Pocket 0.6.0

Sync and loops as in rekordbox, a beat grid that holds, less delay, REC to WAV, a bigger screen that also works upright.

- REC (bar under the waveforms) records the mix to a 24-bit WAV file in Music/FLX4 Pocket.
- Turn the phone upright and the screen restacks without stopping the music. Larger text, each deck's tempo at the top left of its waveform, nothing under the rounded corners or the camera.
- Beat grid to a few thousandths of a BPM, from the whole track (174 is no longer read as 87). Tracks are analysed again the first time they are loaded.
- BEAT SYNC lands on the beat at PLAY and after a hot cue or a search, keeps a jog nudge, keeps its tempo when turned off, syncs 87 with 174. The deck on air is the master. While SYNC is on, the FLX4 tempo fader does nothing.
- Loops: the length shows on 4 BEAT / EXIT and on the BEAT LOOP pads (which now light). BEAT JUMP moves a loop.
- Changed on the FLX4, as in rekordbox: SHIFT + headphones CUE taps the tempo (quantize is now only the Q button on screen); CUE/LOOP CALL outside a loop jumps to a memory cue and stops there.
- Less delay: the status line shows the rate, "fast" and the delay. Please report those three with the FLX4.
- Cleaner highs on pitched tracks, a tighter scratch, fewer redraws when nothing moves.
- FLX4 dark on a direct USB-C cable? Use a USB hub.
